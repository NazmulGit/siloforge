Making genuine espresso at home is the most demanding and most expensive branch of home coffee brewing, but the payoff is a concentrated, syrupy shot that no other method can imitate. Espresso forces water through finely ground, firmly tamped coffee at roughly nine bars of pressure, extracting in under thirty seconds what a pour over accomplishes in four minutes. That intensity magnifies every flaw, so consistency in dose, grind, and distribution matters far more than any single piece of gear.
